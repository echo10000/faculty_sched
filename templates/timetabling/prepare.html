Hard constraints

Conditions a meeting must satisfy

Required meeting frequency and duration, mandatory room specialization, capacity marked as mandatory, operating days and times, recorded hard unavailability, and faculty, section and room overlaps.

Preferences

Qualities the engine tries to improve

Preferred teaching times, faculty rest, compact gaps, meeting distribution and optional room fit. Preferences rank feasible placements; they do not guarantee a placement.

A subject's required room specialization is mandatory unless an explicit class room requirement overrides it. Explicit room type and capacity requirements follow their saved mandatory flags; non-mandatory capacity may still produce a review warning. Availability records are optional inputs: only recorded hard unavailability blocks a meeting. Record weekly frequency and duration for each assigned lecture or laboratory component.
