How room requirements work

A room specialization is a configured room category. Use meaningful categories such as a high-spec computer laboratory or a networking / multimedia laboratory only after staff have verified the rooms' equipment. CampusLoad does not infer equipment from a subject title or a room name.

Mandatory specialization excludes other categories. Preferred specialization allows other rooms; its influence on generation depends on the configured room-fit weight. Mandatory capacity excludes rooms with too few seats; otherwise a shortfall is a warning. If class size is missing, capacity cannot be checked.

Shared requirement: the same offering-level room specialization applies to lecture and laboratory meetings. Requiring a specialized laboratory also restricts the lectures to that category.

Suitability is separate from availability. A suitable room may be occupied or blocked at the requested time. Generation and the existing meeting conflict check enforce availability before a meeting can be saved.
