What the recommendation considers

Current teaching shares, effective workload policy and capacity, active faculty and offerings, and assignments protected by timetable dependencies. Recorded faculty-subject qualifications are positive evidence; an absent record does not prove a faculty member is unqualified.

The recommendation covers faculty choices and workload only. Availability, rooms, and meeting times belong to timetable planning. Accepting a proposal does not generate or approve a timetable.
