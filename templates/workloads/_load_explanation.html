How workload is calculated
Academic teaching units
Lecture and laboratory academic units from assigned classes, multiplied by the instructor's teaching share.
Weekly contact hours
The class's weekly lecture and laboratory hours, multiplied by the teaching share. These describe teaching time, not weighted load or faculty availability.
Weighted teaching load
Lecture units × lecture weight plus laboratory units × laboratory weight, multiplied by the teaching share, summed across the instructor's classes. The target and maximum use this measure.
