ABOUT CAMPUSLOAD

Academic planning, with the details in one place.

CampusLoad brings faculty assignments, workload monitoring, timetable preparation, room allocation, and schedule review into one administrative system.

It offers constraint-based proposals and conflict checks while authorized university personnel retain control of review and approval.
